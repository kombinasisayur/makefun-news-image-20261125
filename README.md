A one-page newspaper for Tuesday 25 November 2026, drawn in dollar green and
black on rice paper. Thirteen full-scene cards lead the page — Iran's claimed
mine strike in the Strait of Hormuz, the new count of more than 700,000
Russian war dead, and the EU's new Russia sanctions — followed by numbered
briefs for a total of 37 dated stories across 9 regions. Every number comes
from the day's reporting; claims are labelled as claims, and Monday's Nvidia
close is tagged as a Monday close.

The program `main.py` uses only the Python standard library and copies the
embedded `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The page itself was composed
locally by `compose_a.py` (the winning variant of a two-variant duel).
Original drawings.
